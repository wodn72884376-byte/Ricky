Editor's Notes
Our Brooklyn is an elegant, minimalist silhouette—with a distinctly New York attitude. Smaller than the 39, the hobo style is crafted of soft leather intricately woven by hand in a process that takes over 22 hours to complete. Featuring a spacious interior with a snap pocket for access to essentials, the streamlined 28 has a comfortable, wide shoulder strap, an easy magnetic snap closure and our tonal Coach graphic.
